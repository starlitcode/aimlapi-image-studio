    prompt tips
    • Name the subject and what the image is for: product photo, poster, diagram.
    • Describe what is visible: materials, lighting, colours, framing, medium.
    • Put exact text in quotes and say where it goes. Ask for no extra text.
    • With references, say what each one is for: "image 1 is the character, image 2 is the style".
    • For edits, say "change only X" and list what must stay the same.
